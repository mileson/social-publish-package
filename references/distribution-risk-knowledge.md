# Copy review boundaries

These checks concern accuracy and relevance, not algorithm predictions or guaranteed reach. No private execution notes, platform anecdotes or historical campaigns are distributed.

| Check | What to inspect | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsupported claims | Rankings, effects, personal testing, revenue and absolute promises | Require evidence or rewrite within known facts |
| Private information | Accounts, contact details, private links and local paths | Remove before public sharing |
| Irrelevant tags | Brands or campaigns unrelated to the video | Omit, explain the content basis |
| Expired campaigns | Dates or participation conditions unknown | Verify current source before use |
| Cross-version selection | User edits differ from draft | Review the latest exported selection |

Do not infer moderation violations from a brand name or link alone. Any claim about current platform rules requires a current primary source. Platform metrics unavailable to the operator are null, not zero. A copy-only check does not verify the video audio or cover.
